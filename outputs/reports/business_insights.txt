======================================================================
  CUSTOMER CHURN & REVENUE INTELLIGENCE — BUSINESS INSIGHTS REPORT
======================================================================

All figures are calculated directly from the cleaned dataset.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1. Month-to-Month Contracts Drive Disproportionate Churn
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FINDING   : Month-to-month customers churn at 42.7% versus 2.8% for two-year contract holders — a 39.9pp gap.
  EVIDENCE  : SQL Query 11 / Contract churn bar chart (Fig 02).
  IMPACT    : Month-to-month customers represent the single largest churn risk pool.
  ACTION    : Launch contract-upgrade incentive campaigns (e.g., 10% discount for switching to annual contracts). Focus on first 3 months.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  2. New Customers (≤12 months) Are Highly Vulnerable
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FINDING   : Customers in their first 12 months churn at 47.4%, versus 9.5% for customers with 48+ months tenure.
  EVIDENCE  : Tenure band churn chart (Fig 03).
  IMPACT    : Early churn destroys CLV before the customer becomes profitable.
  ACTION    : Implement a 90-day onboarding engagement program. Assign customer success touchpoints at 30, 60, and 90 days.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  3. Electronic check Users Churn Most
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FINDING   : Electronic check payment method has the highest churn rate at 45.3%.
  EVIDENCE  : Payment method churn chart (Fig 04).
  IMPACT    : Payment friction and lack of auto-pay commitment correlates with churn.
  ACTION    : Incentivise migration to automatic payment methods. Offer a 5% bill credit for switching to auto-pay.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  4. Fiber Optic Internet Customers Churn at Alarming Rates
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FINDING   : Fiber optic customers churn at 41.9% vs 19.0% for DSL customers.
  EVIDENCE  : Internet service churn chart (Fig 06).
  IMPACT    : High-value fiber customers leaving represents significant MRR loss. Fiber optic is the highest ARPU segment.
  ACTION    : Investigate service-quality issues for fiber subscribers. Deploy proactive support outreach when ticket volume spikes.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  5. Add-On Services Are a Powerful Retention Tool
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FINDING   : Customers with no add-ons churn at 21.4% vs 5.3% for those with all 6 add-ons.
  EVIDENCE  : Add-ons vs churn line chart (Fig 09).
  IMPACT    : Each additional add-on increases product stickiness and reduces churn.
  ACTION    : Cross-sell OnlineSecurity and TechSupport to unprotected high-risk segments. Bundle add-ons into retention offers.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  6. Senior Citizens Face Higher Churn Risk
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FINDING   : Senior citizens churn at 41.7% vs 23.6% for non-seniors.
  EVIDENCE  : Demographic analysis in EDA notebook.
  IMPACT    : Seniors may need more support and simpler pricing clarity.
  ACTION    : Create a 'Senior Care' program with dedicated support lines and simplified billing to reduce friction and confusion.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  7. High-Value Customers Are Not Immune to Churn
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FINDING   : High Value segment churns at 34.1% while Low Value segment churns at 15.8%.
  EVIDENCE  : Value segment churn chart (Fig 07).
  IMPACT    : High-value customers churning has disproportionately large revenue impact per customer.
  ACTION    : Deploy a dedicated high-value customer retention team. Offer exclusive loyalty rewards to High Value customers at risk.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  8. Revenue Lost to Churn Is Substantial
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FINDING   : $2,862,927 in total charges (17.8% of all revenue) was generated by customers who have since churned.
  EVIDENCE  : KPI Summary Table / Revenue analysis SQL.
  IMPACT    : This is historical lost revenue; monthly MRR risk compounds forward.
  ACTION    : Reducing churn rate by even 5 percentage points would protect significant future MRR. Prioritise retention ROI calculations.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  9. Customers Without Partners Churn More
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FINDING   : Customers without partners churn at 33.0% vs 19.7% for those with partners.
  EVIDENCE  : Demographic EDA.
  IMPACT    : Family/shared accounts may feel more switching friction.
  ACTION    : Consider household/family plan offers to single subscribers to increase switching cost and household commitment.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  10. Paperless Billing Correlates with Higher Churn
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FINDING   : Paperless billing customers churn at 33.6% vs 16.3% for paper-billed customers.
  EVIDENCE  : Demographic EDA (billing analysis).
  IMPACT    : Digital-first customers may be more price-sensitive or tech-savvy shoppers.
  ACTION    : Activate personalised digital retention journeys (email/app) for paperless-billing customers flagged as high risk.

======================================================================
  RETENTION STRATEGY RECOMMENDATIONS
======================================================================

Strategy 1 — Contract Upgrade Campaign
  Target   : Month-to-month customers with tenure < 12 months
  Offer    : 10–15% discount on first annual contract
  Channel  : Email + in-app notification

Strategy 2 — Onboarding Success Program
  Target   : All new customers (tenure ≤ 3 months)
  Offer    : Proactive check-in calls at 30/60/90 days
  Goal     : Reduce New segment churn by 20%

Strategy 3 — Add-On Cross-Sell Retention
  Target   : Active customers with NumAddOns = 0 or 1
  Offer    : Free 3-month trial of OnlineSecurity
  Rationale: Every add-on reduces churn probability

Strategy 4 — High-Value At-Risk Program
  Target   : BusinessSegment = 'High Value / High Risk'
  Offer    : Dedicated account manager + loyalty reward
  Channel  : Direct phone outreach

Strategy 5 — Payment Method Migration
  Target   : Electronic check users with high churn risk
  Offer    : $10 bill credit for switching to auto-pay
  Rationale: Auto-pay reduces voluntary cancellation friction
